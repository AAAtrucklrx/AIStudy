### 4.2.4 转置、共轭与迹

定义4.2.5. 将矩阵 $A=(a_{ij})_{m\times n}$ 的行列互换, 得到的矩阵$$\left( \begin{array}{c c c c} a _ {1 1} & a _ {2 1} & \dots & a _ {m 1} \\ a _ {1 2} & a _ {2 2} & \dots & a _ {m 2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \dots & \vdots \\ a _ {1 n} & a _ {2 n} & \dots & a _ {m n} \end{array} \right),\tag{4.13}$$称为A的转置矩阵, 记作 $A^{T}=(a_{ji})_{n\times m}$ .将复矩阵A的每个元素换成它的共轭复数,得到的矩阵$$\left( \begin{array}{c c c c} \overline {{a _ {1 1}}} & \overline {{a _ {1 2}}} & \dots & \overline {{a _ {1 n}}} \\ \overline {{a _ {2 1}}} & \overline {{a _ {2 2}}} & \dots & a _ {2 n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \dots & \vdots \\ \overline {{a _ {n 1}}} & \overline {{a _ {n 2}}} & \dots & \overline {{a _ {m n}}} \end{array} \right),\tag{4.14}$$称为A的共轭矩阵, 记作 $\overline{A}=(\overline{a_{ij}})_{m\times n}$ . $n$ 阶方阵 $A$ 的对角元之和$$a _ {1 1} + a _ {2 2} + \dots + a _ {n n}\tag{4.15}$$称为A的迹, 记作 $\mathrm{tr}(A)$ .例4.2.10. 设 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + i & 2 - i \\ 3 - i & 4 + i \end{pmatrix}$, 计算 $A^T$、$\overline{A}$、$\operatorname{tr}(A)$.解. $A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + i & 3 - i \\ 2 - i & 4 + i \end{pmatrix}, \overline{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 - i & 2 + i \\ 3 + i & 4 - i \end{pmatrix}, \operatorname{tr}(A) = 5 + 2i.$定理4.2.6. 矩阵的转置运算具有以下性质:
(1) $(A + B)^T = A^T + B^T$
(2) $(\lambda A)^T = \lambda A^T$
(3) $(AB)^T = B^T A^T$
(4) $(A^{-1})^T = (A^T)^{-1}$其中A,B是使运算有意义的矩阵, $\lambda$ 是数.证明. 性质(1)与(2)可通过矩阵运算的定义直接验证, 留作习题. 下证性质(3)与(4). 设 $A=(a_{ij})_{m\times n}, B=(b_{ij})_{n\times p}$ , 则有$$\left(\left(A B\right) ^ {T}\right) _ {i j} = (A B) _ {j i} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {j k} b _ {k i},$$ $$\left(B ^ {T} A ^ {T}\right) _ {i j} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} b _ {k i} a _ {j k}.$$上述两和式相等, 故 $(AB)^{T}=B^{T}A^{T}$ . 特别地, 对可逆方阵A, $$(A ^ {- 1}) ^ {T} A ^ {T} = (A A ^ {- 1}) ^ {T} = I, \quad A ^ {T} (A ^ {- 1}) ^ {T} = (A ^ {- 1} A) ^ {T} = I.$$根据定义4.2.4, $(A^{-1})^{T} = (A^{T})^{-1}$.定理4.2.7. 矩阵的迹具有以下性质:
(1) $\operatorname{tr}(A + B) = \operatorname{tr}(A) + \operatorname{tr}(B)$ §4.2 矩阵的运算
(2) $\operatorname{tr}(\lambda A) = \lambda \operatorname{tr}(A)$
(3) $\operatorname{tr}(A^T) = \operatorname{tr}(A), \operatorname{tr}(\overline{A}) = \overline{\operatorname{tr}(A)}$
(4) $\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)$其中A,B是使运算有意义的矩阵, $\lambda$ 是数.证明. 性质(1)、(2)、(3)可通过矩阵运算的定义直接验证, 留作习题. 下证性质(4).设 $A=(a_{ij})_{m\times n},B=(b_{ij})_{n\times m}$ ，则有$$\operatorname{tr} (A B) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} (A B) _ {i i} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i j} b _ {j i},$$ $$\operatorname{tr} (B A) = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (B A) _ {j j} = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} b _ {j i} a _ {i j}.$$通过交换求和的次序, 以上两式相等, 故 $\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)$.例4.2.11. 设复矩阵A满足 $\mathrm{tr}(A\overline{A}^{T})=0,$ 证明:A=O.证明. 设 $A = (a_{ij})_{m \times n}, A\overline{A}^T = (b_{ij})_{m \times m}$. 由$$0 = \operatorname{tr} (A \overline {{A}} ^ {T}) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} b _ {i i} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i j} \overline {{a _ {i j}}} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} | a _ {i j} | ^ {2}$$可知所有 $a_{ij}=0$ ，即A=O。
