### 1.4.2 直角坐标系下向量积的计算

设 $[O; \mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}]$ 为一个直角坐标系. 由于 $\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}$ 为两两垂直的单位向量且满足右手法则, 由向量积的定义知$$\begin{array}{l l} \mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {i} = \mathbf {0}, & \mathbf {j} \times \mathbf {j} = \mathbf {0}, \quad \mathbf {k} \times \mathbf {k} = \mathbf {0} \\ \mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {j} = \mathbf {k}, & \mathbf {j} \times \mathbf {k} = \mathbf {i}, \quad \mathbf {k} \times \mathbf {i} = \mathbf {j}. \end{array}$$给定两个向量$$\mathbf {a} = a _ {1} \mathbf {i} + a _ {2} \mathbf {j} + a _ {3} \mathbf {k}, \quad \mathbf {b} = b _ {1} \mathbf {i} + b _ {2} \mathbf {j} + b _ {3} \mathbf {k},$$ $$\begin{array}{r l} \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b} & = (a _ {1} \mathbf {i} + a _ {2} \mathbf {j} + a _ {3} \mathbf {k}) \times (b _ {1} \mathbf {i} + b _ {2} \mathbf {j} + b _ {3} \mathbf {k}) \\ & = a _ {1} b _ {1} (\mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {i}) + a _ {1} b _ {2} (\mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {j}) + a _ {1} b _ {3} (\mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {k}) \\ & \quad + a _ {2} b _ {1} (\mathbf {j} \times \mathbf {i}) + a _ {2} b _ {2} (\mathbf {i} \times \mathbf {j}) + a _ {2} b _ {3} (\mathbf {j} \times \mathbf {k}) \\ & \quad + a _ {3} b _ {1} (\mathbf {k} \times \mathbf {i}) + a _ {3} b _ {2} (\mathbf {k} \times \mathbf {j}) + a _ {3} b _ {3} (\mathbf {k} \times \mathbf {k}) \\ & = (a _ {2} b _ {3} - a _ {3} b _ {2}) \mathbf {i} + (a _ {3} b _ {1} - a _ {1} b _ {3}) \mathbf {j} + (a _ {1} b _ {2} - a _ {2} b _ {1}) \mathbf {k} \end{array}$$为了便于记忆上式, 我们引入 2 阶和 3 阶行列式的概念.把 4 个数排成两行两列, 在两边加上两条竖线, 就得到一个 2 阶行列式. 它代表一个数, 其运算规则是$$\left| \begin{array}{c c} a _ {1} & a _ {2} \\ b _ {1} & b _ {2} \end{array} \right| = a _ {1} b _ {2} - a _ {2} b _ {1}.$$类似地, 9 个数排成 3 行 3 列, 就得到一个 3 阶行列式. 它的运算规则是$$\begin{array}{l} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} a _ {1} & a _ {2} & a _ {3} \\ b _ {1} & b _ {2} & b _ {3} \\ c _ {1} & c _ {2} & c _ {3} \end{array} \right| = a _ {1} \left| \begin{array}{c c} b _ {2} & b _ {3} \\ c _ {2} & c _ {3} \end{array} \right| - a _ {2} \left| \begin{array}{c c} b _ {1} & b _ {3} \\ c _ {1} & c _ {3} \end{array} \right| + a _ {3} \left| \begin{array}{c c} b _ {1} & b _ {3} \\ c _ {1} & c _ {3} \end{array} \right| \\ = a _ {1} b _ {2} c _ {3} + a _ {2} b _ {3} c _ {1} + a _ {3} b _ {1} c _ {2} - a _ {1} b _ {3} c _ {2} - a _ {2} b _ {1} c _ {3} - a _ {3} b _ {2} c _ {1}. \end{array}$$关于行列式的详细介绍,请参看第三章.利用行列式的语言,前面关于向量积的运算公式可以改写为$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b} & = \left| \begin{array}{c c} a _ {2} & a _ {3} \\ b _ {2} & b _ {3} \end{array} \right| \mathbf {i} - \left| \begin{array}{c c} a _ {1} & a _ {3} \\ b _ {1} & b _ {3} \end{array} \right| \mathbf {j} + \left| \begin{array}{c c} a _ {1} & a _ {2} \\ b _ {1} & b _ {2} \end{array} \right| \mathbf {k} \\ & = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \mathbf {i} & \mathbf {j} & \mathbf {k} \\ a _ {1} & a _ {2} & a _ {3} \\ b _ {1} & b _ {2} & b _ {3} \end{array} \right|. \end{array}\tag{1.26}$$例1.4.1. 求垂直于向量  $\mathbf{a}=(-1,2,1)$  和  $\mathbf{b}=(1,0,3)$  的单位向量.解.  $a \times b$  就是一个垂直于 a 和 b 的向量, 而$$\begin{array}{l} \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \mathbf {i} & \mathbf {j} & \mathbf {k} \\ - 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{array} \right| \mathbf {i} - \left| \begin{array}{c c} - 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{array} \right| \mathbf {j} + \left| \begin{array}{c c} - 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{array} \right| \mathbf {k} \\ = 6 \mathbf {i} + 4 \mathbf {j} - 2 \mathbf {k}. \\ | \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b} | = \sqrt {6 ^ {2} + 4 ^ {2} + (- 2) ^ {2}} = 2 \sqrt {1 4} \end{array}$$因此所求的单位向量为$$\pm \frac {\mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b}}{| \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {b} |} = \pm \frac {1}{\sqrt {1 4}} (3, 2, - 1).$$与例 1.3.2 相比, 这里的解法更为简单.例1.4.2. 设三角形的三个定点为 $A(1,1,2), B(-2,0,3), C(2,4,5)$, 求 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积以及 $BC$ 边上的高 $h$.解. 设 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积为 $S$, 由向量积的几何意义知 $S = \frac{1}{2} |\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}|$. 而 $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-3, -1, 1), \overrightarrow{AC} = (1, 3, 3)$, 所以$$\overrightarrow {A B} \times \overrightarrow {A C} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \mathbf {i} & \mathbf {j} & \mathbf {k} \\ - 3 & - 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & 3 \end{array} \right| = - 6 \mathbf {i} + 1 0 \mathbf {j} - 8 \mathbf {k}.$$ $$S = \frac {1}{2} \sqrt {(- 6) ^ {2} + 1 0 ^ {2} + (- 8) ^ {2}} = 5 \sqrt {2}.$$另一方面, 由 $S = \frac{1}{2} |\overrightarrow{BC}| \cdot h$ 及 $\overrightarrow{BC} = (4,4,2)$ 可解得 $h = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{3}$.
