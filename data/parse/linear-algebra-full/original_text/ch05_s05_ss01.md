### 5.5.1 线性方程组解的存在性与唯一性

根据前几节的结论, 我们可以给出线性方程组解的存在性与唯一性条件.定理5.5.1. 设 $A \in F^{m \times n}$ 为 $m \times n$ 阶矩阵, $b \in F^{m}$ 为m维列向量. 则线性方程组$$A \mathbf {x} = \mathbf {b}\tag{5.15}$$有解的充要条件是 $\operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(A, \mathbf{b})$。线性方程组有唯一解的充要条件是 $\operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(A, \mathbf{b}) = n$。
证明. 设矩阵 $A$ 的列为 $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n, \mathbf{x} = (x_1, \ldots, x_n)^T$. 则线性方程组 $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ 等价于$$x _ {1} \mathbf {a} _ {1} + x _ {2} \mathbf {a} _ {2} + \dots + x _ {n} \mathbf {a} _ {n} = \mathbf {b}.$$因此, 线性方程组有解 $\Leftrightarrow \mathbf{b} \in \langle \mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n \rangle \Leftrightarrow \langle \mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n \rangle = \langle \mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n, \mathbf{b} \rangle \cdot \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n) = \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n, \mathbf{b}) \Leftrightarrow \operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(A, \mathbf{b}).$ $\Leftrightarrow$线性方程组有唯一解 $\Leftrightarrow \mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ 线性无关，且 $\operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(A, \mathbf{b}) \Leftrightarrow \operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(A, \mathbf{b}) = n$.推论5.5.1. 齐次线性方程组$$A \mathbf {x} = \mathbf {0}\tag{5.16}$$有非零解的充要条件是 $\operatorname{rank}(A) < n$. 特别地, 若 $A$ 为 $n$ 阶方阵, 则齐次线性方程组有非零解当且仅当 $\det(A) = 0$.
