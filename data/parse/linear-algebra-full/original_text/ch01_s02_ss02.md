### 1.2.2 向量的坐标运算

设在空间中取定了一个仿射坐标系  $[O; e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}]$ . 由于向量与其坐标之间存在一一对应的关系, 向量的运算可以转化为其坐标间的运算. §1.2 坐标系图1.5设  $\mathbf{a}=(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}),\mathbf{b}=(y_{1},y_{2},y_{3}),\lambda$  为一个实数, 则$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathbf {a} + \mathbf {b} & = (x _ {1} \mathbf {e} _ {1} + x _ {2} \mathbf {e} _ {2} + x _ {3} \mathbf {e} _ {3}) + (y _ {1} \mathbf {e} _ {1} + y _ {2} \mathbf {e} _ {2} + y _ {3} \mathbf {e} _ {3}) \\ & = (x _ {1} + y _ {1}) \mathbf {e} _ {1} + (x _ {2} + y _ {2}) \mathbf {e} _ {2} + (x _ {3} + y _ {3}) \mathbf {e} _ {3}, \\ \lambda \mathbf {a} & = \lambda (x _ {1} \mathbf {e} _ {1} + x _ {2} \mathbf {e} _ {2} + x _ {3} \mathbf {e} _ {3}) = \lambda x _ {1} \mathbf {e} _ {1} + \lambda x _ {2} \mathbf {e} _ {2} + \lambda x _ {3} \mathbf {e} _ {3}. \end{array}$$所以我们有下面的坐标计算公式:
$$(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}) + (y _ {1}, y _ {2}, y _ {3}) = (x _ {1} + y _ {1}, x _ {2} + y _ {2}, x _ {3} + y _ {3}),$$ $$\lambda \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(\lambda x _ {1}, \lambda x _ {2}, \lambda x _ {3}\right).\tag{1.10}$$ (1.11)
例1.2.2. 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1},z_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2},z_{2})$ 为空间中两点, 若点 $P$ 将 $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 分割成定比 $\lambda$, 即 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{PB}$, 求分点 $P$ 的坐标.解. 由 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{PB}$, 知 $\overrightarrow{OP} - \overrightarrow{OA} = \lambda (\overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OP})$. 因此$$\overrightarrow {O P} = \frac {1}{1 + \lambda} \overrightarrow {O A} + \frac {\lambda}{1 + \lambda} \overrightarrow {O B} = \left(\frac {x _ {1} + \lambda x _ {2}}{1 + \lambda}, \frac {y _ {1} + \lambda y _ {2}}{1 + \lambda}, \frac {z _ {1} + \lambda z _ {2}}{1 + \lambda}\right).$$特别地, AB 中点的坐标为  $((x_{1}+x_{2})/2, (y_{1}+y_{2})/2, (z_{1}+z_{2})/2)$ .例1.2.3. 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1},z_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2},z_{2}),C(x_{3},y_{3},z_{3})$ ，求 $\triangle ABC$ 重心 $G$ 的坐标解. 设 BC 边的中点为 D, 则 D 点的坐标为$$\left(\frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2}, \frac {y _ {2} + y _ {3}}{2}, \frac {z _ {2} + z _ {3}}{2}\right).$$由例 1.1.3 知,  $\overrightarrow{AG} = 2\overrightarrow{GD}$ , 利用定比分点公式得$$x = \frac {x _ {1} + 2 \frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2}}{1 + 2} = \frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{3},$$同理$$y = \frac {y _ {1} + y _ {2} + y _ {3}}{3}, \quad z = \frac {z _ {1} + z _ {2} + z _ {3}}{3}.$$所以, 重心 G 的坐标为$$\left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{3}, \frac {y _ {1} + y _ {2} + y _ {3}}{3}, \frac {z _ {1} + z _ {2} + z _ {3}}{3}\right).$$
