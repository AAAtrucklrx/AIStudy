## §3.4 Cramer法则

在第一章中, 我们提出了求线性方程组的公式解的问题. 在本章的第0节, 我们给给出了二元一次方程组及三元一次方程组的公式解. 本节中, 我们将给出求解具有 $n$ 个方程和 $n$ 个未知数的线性方程组的公式解的法则-Cramer 法则. 为此, 我们先给出一个有用的结论.定理3.4.1. 设 $A=(a_{ij})$ 为n阶方阵, 则$$\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i k} A _ {j k} = \det (A) \delta_ {i j}, \quad \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {k i} A _ {k j} = \det (A) \delta_ {i j}, \qquad i, j = 1, 2, \dots , n,\tag{3.15}$$其中 $\delta_{ij}$ 为Kronecker记号$$\delta_ {i j} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & i = j \\ 0, & i \neq j \end{array} \right.$$证明. 我们只证 $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{ik} A_{jk} = \det(A) \delta_{ij}$, 也就是$$\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i k} A _ {i k} = \det (A), \quad \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i k} A _ {j k} = 0, i \neq j.$$另一式同理可得. $\operatorname{det}(A)$ 按第 $i$ 行展开显然有 $\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{ik} A_{ik} = \operatorname{det}(A)$. 下设 $i \neq j$, 记矩阵 $B$ 为将 $A$ 的第 $j$ 行用第 $i$ 行替换所得矩阵. 由于 $B$ 的第 $i, j$ 两行元素相同, 由行列式的性质(4), $\operatorname{det}(B) = 0$. 另一方面, 将行列式 $\operatorname{det}(B)$ 按第 $j$ 行展开得$$\det (B) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i k} A _ {j k} = 0.$$我们以 $n = 3$ 为例解释一下定理3.4.1的结论.由(3.15)式得$$a _ {1 1} A _ {1 1} + a _ {1 2} A _ {1 2} + a _ {1 3} A _ {1 3} = \det (A), \quad a _ {2 1} A _ {1 1} + a _ {2 2} A _ {1 2} + a _ {2 3} A _ {1 3} = 0, \quad a _ {3 1} A _ {1 1} + a _ {3 2} A _ {1 2} + a _ {3 3} A _ {1 3} = 0.$$分别用 $a_{1},a_{2},a_{3}$ 表示三阶行列式 $\det(A)$ 的三个行向量. 则 $(A_{11},A_{12},A_{13})=\mathbf{a}_{2}\times\mathbf{a}_{3}$ ，于是上面几个式子等价于$$\det (A) = \mathbf {a} _ {1} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3}, \quad \mathbf {a} _ {2} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3} = 0, \quad \mathbf {a} _ {3} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3} = 0.$$利用上述定理的结论, 我们可以方便地证明Cramer法则.定理3.4.2 (Cramer法则). 当系数矩阵 $A = (a_{ij})_{n \times n}$ 的行列式不等于零时, 线性方程组$$\left\{ \begin{array}{c} a _ {1 1} x _ {1} + a _ {1 2} x _ {2} + \dots + a _ {1 n} x _ {n} = b _ {1} \\ a _ {2 1} x _ {1} + a _ {2 2} x _ {2} + \dots + a _ {2 n} x _ {n} = b _ {2} \\ \dots \quad \dots \quad \dots \\ a _ {n 1} x _ {1} + a _ {n 2} x _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n n} x _ {n} = b _ {n} \end{array} \right.$$有唯一解$$(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {n}) = \left(\frac {\Delta_ {1}}{\Delta}, \frac {\Delta_ {2}}{\Delta}, \dots , \frac {\Delta_ {n}}{\Delta}\right),$$其中 $\Delta = \det(A), \Delta_i$ 是将 $A$ 的第 $i$ 列换成常数项后所得方阵的行列式, $i = 1,2,\ldots,n$. 证. 将线性方程组的每个方程分别乘 $A_{1i}, A_{2i}, \ldots, A_{ni}$, 然后求和得$$\sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} A _ {j i} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {j k} x _ {k} = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} A _ {j i} b _ {j}.$$上式左边为$$\sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} A _ {j i} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {j k} x _ {k} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \left(\sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} a _ {j k} A _ {j i}\right) x _ {k} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \delta_ {k i} \det (A) x _ {k} = \Delta \cdot x _ {i}.$$而行列式 $\Delta_{i}$ 按第i列展开得到 $\Delta_{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}A_{ji}b_{j}$ . 因此当 $\Delta\neq0$ 时, $x_{i}=\Delta_{i}/\Delta,i=1,2,\ldots,n$ . 定理证毕. □我们以 $n = 3$ 来理解一下上面证明的思路. 当 $n = 3$ 时, 原方程组可以写成向量形式$$x _ {1} \mathbf {a} _ {1} + x _ {2} \mathbf {a} _ {2} + x _ {3} \mathbf {a} _ {3} = \mathbf {b},$$ $$\mathbf {a} _ {1} = \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {1 1} \\ a _ {2 1} \\ a _ {3 1} \end{array} \right), \quad \mathbf {a} _ {2} = \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {1 2} \\ a _ {2 2} \\ a _ {3 2} \end{array} \right), \quad \mathbf {a} _ {3} = \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {1 3} \\ a _ {2 3} \\ a _ {3 3} \end{array} \right), \quad \mathbf {b} = \left( \begin{array}{c} b _ {1} \\ b _ {2} \\ b _ {3} \end{array} \right).$$对方程两边点乘 $a_{2}\times a_{3}$ 可得$$\mathbf {a} _ {1} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3} x _ {1} = \mathbf {b} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3}.$$ $$x _ {1} = \frac {\mathbf {b} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3}}{\mathbf {a} _ {1} \cdot \mathbf {a} _ {2} \times \mathbf {a} _ {3}} = \frac {\Delta_ {1}}{\Delta}.$$同理, 可以得到 $x_{2}, x_{3}$ 的表示式.例3.4.1. 用Cramer法则解线性方程组$$\left\{ \begin{array}{c} x _ {2} + x _ {3} + x _ {4} = 1 \\ x _ {1} \quad + x _ {3} + x _ {4} = 2 \\ x _ {1} + x _ {2} \quad + x _ {4} = 3 \\ x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3} \quad = 4 \end{array} \right.$$ $$\Delta = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right| = - 3, \quad \Delta_ {1} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 4 & 1 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right| = - 7, \quad \Delta_ {2} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 4 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right| = - 4,$$ $$\Delta_ {3} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 4 & 0 \end{array} \right| = - 1, \quad \Delta_ {4} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & 4 \end{array} \right| = 2.$$故方程组的解为$$x _ {1} = \frac {7}{3}, x _ {2} = \frac {4}{3}, x _ {3} = \frac {1}{3}, x _ {4} = - \frac {2}{3}.$$一般来说, Cramer法则主要用于理论分析, 除非相关的行列式容易计算, 否则用Cramer法则求解方程的运算量非常巨大.
