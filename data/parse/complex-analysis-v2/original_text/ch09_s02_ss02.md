### 9.2.2 相似定理

设 $\mathsf{L}[f(t)] = F(p)$，则对于任一常数 $\alpha > 0$，有
$$\mathrm{L} [ f (\alpha t) ] = \frac {1}{\alpha} F \left(\frac {p}{\alpha}\right), \quad \operatorname{Re} p > \alpha c.$$
事实上，由定义有
$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathrm{L} [ f (\alpha t) ] & = \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} f (\alpha t) \mathrm{e} ^ {- p t} \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{\alpha} \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} f (\xi) \mathrm{e} ^ {- p \xi / \alpha} \mathrm{d} \xi \\ & = \frac {1}{\alpha} F \left(\frac {p}{\alpha}\right). \end{array}$$
