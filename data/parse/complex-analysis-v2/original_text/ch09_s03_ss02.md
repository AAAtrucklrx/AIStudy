### 9.3.2 拉氏变换的反演公式

定理3 设 $f(t)$ 满足条件1)与2)，$L[f(t)] = F(p)$. 则对任意取定的 $\sigma > c$，在 $f(t)$ 的连续点处，有
$$f (t) = \frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {\sigma - \mathrm{i} m} ^ {\sigma + \mathrm{i} m} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \mathrm{d} p.\tag{9.10}$$
上式右端的积分是沿自下而上的直线 $\mathrm{Re}p = \sigma$ 进行的.
证 令 $p = \sigma + \mathrm{i}s$，则
$$\begin{array}{r l} F (p) & = \mathrm{L} [ f (t) ] = F [ f (t) h (t) \mathrm{e} ^ {- s t} ] \\ & = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} [ f (t) h (t) \mathrm{e} ^ {- s t} ] \mathrm{e} ^ {- s t} \mathrm{d} t. \end{array}$$
于是，由傅氏反变换公式，在 $f(t)$ 的连续点处，有
$$f (t) h (t) \mathrm{e} ^ {- s t} = \frac {1}{2 \pi} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{i} s t} \mathrm{d} s$$
$$f (t) = \frac {1}{2 \pi} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} F (p) e ^ {\mu} d s.$$
当 $s$ 从 $-\infty$ 变到 $+\infty$ 时，$p = \sigma + \mathrm{i}s$ 就在直线 $\operatorname{Re} p = \sigma$ 上从 $\sigma - \mathrm{i}\infty$ 变到 $\sigma + \mathrm{i}\infty$。再注意到 $\mathrm{d}p = \mathrm{i}ds$，即得
$$f (t) = \frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {e ^ {- i \infty}} ^ {e ^ {+ i \infty}} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mu} \mathrm{d} p.$$
这条定理所给出的拉氏变换的反演公式，称为傅立叶-梅林(Fourier-Mellin)公式.下面的定理，进一步给出了用留数计算本函数的方法.
定理 4 设  $F(p)$  除在半平面  $Re p \leqslant \sigma$  内有奇点  $p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{n}$  外，在 p 平面内处处解析，当  $p \to \infty$  时， $F(p) \to 0$ ，且积分
$$\int_ {\sigma - i m} ^ {\sigma + i m} F (p) \mathrm{d} p, \quad \sigma > c$$
绝对收敛.则
1)  $F(p)$  是函数
$$f (t) = \frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {s - \mathrm{i} *} ^ {s + \mathrm{i} *} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{i} p} \mathrm{d} p$$
$$2) \frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {s - \mathrm{i} = 0} ^ {s + \mathrm{i} = 0} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mu t} \mathrm{d} p = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \mathbf {R e s} [ F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mu t}, p _ {k} ], \text {即}$$
图9.7
$$f (t) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mu}, p _ {k} ], \quad t > 0.\tag{9.11}$$
证 作如图9.7所示的闭路 $C = L + C_R$，因 $l = \sqrt{R^2 - \sigma^2}$，故当 $R \to +\infty$ 时，$l \to +\infty$。
1) 这里所给的积分应取柯西主值，有
$$\begin{array}{l}\frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {\sigma - i \infty} ^ {\sigma + i \infty} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \mathrm{d} p\\= \lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {\sigma - i t} ^ {\sigma + i t} \left[ \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {p (t - \tau)} f (\tau) h (\tau) \mathrm{d} \tau \right] \mathrm{d} p.\end{array}$$
令 $p = \sigma +\mathrm{i}s$ ，取 $\sigma >0$ 使 $\mathbf{e}^{-\sigma}\mathbf{f}(\tau)h(\tau)$ 绝对可积，代入上式，得到
$$\begin{array}{l}\lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {a t}}{2 \pi} \int_ {- t} ^ {t} \mathrm{e} ^ {i w} \left\{\int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} - i s \tau [ \mathrm{e} ^ {- a t} f (\tau) h (\tau) ] \mathrm{d} \tau \right\} \mathrm{d} s\\= \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {a t}}{2 \pi} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {- a t} f (s) h (s) \mathrm{e} ^ {i u} \mathrm{d} s\\= \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {a t}}{2 \pi} \cdot 2 \pi \mathrm{e} ^ {- a t} f (t) h (t)\\= f (t), \quad t \geqslant 0,\end{array}$$
其中，应用到对 $e^{-\alpha}f(\tau)h(\tau)$ 作傅氏变换；
2）当 R 充分大时，可使所有  $p_{k}, k = 1, 2, \cdots, n$  点都在闭路 C 内。因  $p_{k}$  即是  $F(p)e^{\mu t}$  的所有有限奇点，由留数定理，得
$$\begin{array}{l} 2 \pi \mathrm{i} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t}, p _ {k} ] = \int_ {C} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \mathrm{d} p \\ = \int_ {C _ {\mathrm{R}}} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \mathrm{d} p + \int_ {\mathrm{L}} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \mathrm{d} p. \end{array}\tag{9.12}$$
由约当引理(令 p = iz 就化成已知的形式)，有
$$\lim _ {R \rightarrow \infty} \int_ {C _ {R}} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {\mu} \mathrm{d} p = 0.$$
于是，在(9.12)式两边令 $R \to \infty$ 取极限，即得
$$\frac {1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}} \int_ {\sigma - i =} ^ {\sigma + i =} F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {i p} \mathrm{d} p = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {i p}, p _ {k} ].$$
当 $F(p)$ 是有理真分式时，$F(p)e^{p}$ 在极点的留数很容易计算，因而这时用(9.11)式计算本函数很方便。
例18 求 $F(p) = \frac{2p^2 - 4p}{(2p + 1)(p^2 + 1)}$ 的本函数.
解 $B(p) = (2p + 1)(p^2 + 1) = (2p + 1)(p - \mathrm{i})(p + \mathrm{i})$ 有三个单零点 $-1/2, \mathrm{i}, -\mathrm{i}$, 它们都是 $F(p)$ 的一级极点. 因 $B'(p) = 6p^2 + 2p + 2$, 所以
$$\begin{array}{r l} f (t) & = \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) e ^ {i \pi}, - 1 / 2 ] + \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) e ^ {i \pi}, i ] + \operatorname{Res} [ F (p) e ^ {i \pi}, - i ] \\ & = \left[ \frac {2 p ^ {2} - 4 p}{6 p ^ {2} + 2 p + 2} e ^ {i \pi} \right] _ {p = - 1 / 2} + \left[ \frac {2 p ^ {3} - 4 p}{6 p ^ {2} + 2 p + 2} e ^ {i \pi} \right] _ {p = i} + \left[ \frac {2 p ^ {3} - 4 p}{6 p ^ {2} + 2 p + 2} e ^ {i \pi} \right] _ {p = - i} \\ & = e ^ {- i t / 2} + \frac {e ^ {i t}}{i} + \frac {e ^ {- i t}}{i} \\ & = e ^ {- i t / 2} - 2 \sin t. \end{array}$$
例19 求 $f(t) = \mathrm{L}^{-1}\left[\frac{2p^2 + 3p + 3}{(p + 1)(p + 3)^3}\right]$.
解 $p = -1$ 及 $p = -3$ 分别是 $F(p) = \frac{2p^2 + 3p + 3}{(p + 1)(p + 3)^3}\mathrm{e}^{pr}$ 的一级和三级极点，于是
$$\begin{array}{r l}f (t)&= \operatorname{Res} [ F (p), - 1 ] + \operatorname{Res} [ F (p), - 3 ]\\&= \lim _ {p \rightarrow - 1} \frac {2 p ^ {2} + 3 p + 3}{(p + 3) ^ {3}} \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} + \lim _ {p \rightarrow - 3} \frac {1}{2} \frac {\mathrm{d} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{d} p ^ {2}} \left[ \frac {2 p ^ {2} + 3 p + 3}{p + 1} \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \right]\\&= \frac {1}{4} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t} + \left(- 3 t ^ {2} + \frac {3}{2} t - \frac {1}{4}\right) \mathrm{e} ^ {- 3 t}.\end{array}$$
例20 解方程组
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{\mathrm{d} t} - x - 2 y = t \\ - 2 x + \frac {\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} t} - y = t \\ x (0) = 2, y (0) = 4. \end{array} \right.$$
解 作拉氏变换 $X(p) = \mathrm{L}[x(t)]$，$Y(p) = \mathrm{L}[y(t)]$，则
$$\mathrm{L} \left[ \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{\mathrm{d} t} \right] = p X (p) - 2, \mathrm{L} \left[ \frac {\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} t} \right] = p Y (p) - 4.$$
$$\mathrm{L} [ t ] = \frac {1}{p ^ {2}},$$
于是原方程组变换为
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} (p - 1) X - 2 Y = \frac {1}{p ^ {2}} + 2 \\ - 2 X + (p - 1) Y = \frac {1}{p ^ {2}} + 4. \end{array} \right.\tag{9.13}$$
(9.14)
将方程(9.13) + (9.14)，得
$$(p - 3) (X + Y) = \frac {2}{p ^ {2}} + 6, \quad X + Y = \frac {6 p ^ {2} + 2}{p ^ {2} (p - 3)}.\tag{9.15}$$
将方程(9.13)-(9.14)，得
$$(p + 1) (X - Y) = - 2, \quad X - Y = \frac {- 2}{p + 1}.\tag{9.16}$$
由(9.15)，(9.16)式又可求得
$$X = \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p ^ {2} (p - 3)} - \frac {1}{p + 1}, \quad Y = \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p ^ {2} (p - 3)} + \frac {1}{p + 1}.$$
最后得
$$\begin{array}{r l} x (t) & = \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ X ] = \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p ^ {2} (p - 3)} \right] - \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{p + 1} \right] \\ & = \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p ^ {2}} \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \Big | _ {p = 3} + \frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} p} \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p - 3} \mathrm{e} ^ {p t} \Big | _ {p = 0} - \mathrm{e} ^ {- t} \\ & = \frac {2 8}{9} \mathrm{e} ^ {3 t} - \frac {t}{3} - \frac {1}{9} - \mathrm{e} ^ {- t}, \end{array}$$
$$\begin{array}{r l} y (t) & = \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ Y ] = \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {3 p ^ {2} + 1}{p ^ {2} (p - 3)} \right] + \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{p + 1} \right] \\ & = \frac {2 8}{9} \mathrm{e} ^ {3 t} - \frac {t}{3} - \frac {1}{9} + \mathrm{e} ^ {- t}. \end{array}$$
上面集中讨论了如何求有理真分式 $\frac{A(p)}{B(p)}$ 的本函数.在有些场合还会遇到求形如
$$F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {- p \tau}, \quad \tau > 0$$
的像函数的本函数,它可以利用延迟定理来求.事实上,若 $L[f(t)]=F(p)$ ,则
$$\mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ F (p) \mathrm{e} ^ {- \tau p} ] = f (t - \tau) h (t - \tau),\tag{9.17}$$
例21 求 $\mathrm{L}^{-1}\left[\frac{1 - \mathrm{e}^{-4p}}{p^2 - 2}\right]$.
$$\mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{p ^ {2} - 2} \right] = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \sinh \sqrt {2} t.$$
故由(9.17)式得
$$\mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} \left[ \frac {1 - \mathrm{e} ^ {- 4 p}}{p ^ {2} - 2} \right] = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \left[ \sinh \sqrt {2} t - \sinh \sqrt {2} (t - 4) h (t - 4) \right]$$
$$= \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 0, & t <   0 \\ \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \sinh \sqrt {2} t, & 0 \leqslant t <   4 \\ \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} [ \sinh \sqrt {2} t - \sinh \sqrt {2} (t - 4) ], & 4 \leqslant t. \end{array} \right.$$
