### 1.1.2 共轭复数

共轭复数的运用，在复数运算上有着重大意义.先把它的一些运算性质罗列如下：
1)  $\bar{z} = z.$
$$z + \bar {z} = 2 \mathrm{Re} z, z - \bar {z} = 2 \mathrm{i} \mathrm{Im} z.$$
$$3) \overline {{z _ {1} \pm z _ {2}}} = \bar {z} _ {1} \pm \bar {z} _ {2}.$$
$$4) \overline {{{z _ {1} z _ {2}}}} = \bar {z} _ {1} \cdot \bar {z} _ {2}, \left(\frac {\bar {z} _ {1}}{\bar {z} _ {2}}\right) = \frac {\bar {z} _ {1}}{\bar {z} _ {2}}.$$
$$z \bar {z} = (\operatorname{Re} z) ^ {2} + (\operatorname{Im} z) ^ {2} = | z | ^ {2}.$$
这些性质都不难证明，留给读者做练习。此外，由性质2)的第2个式子可知，复数z是实数的充要条件是 $z=\bar{z}$ ；由第1个式子得知，z是纯虚数的充要条件是 $z=-\bar{z}$ ，
且 $z \neq 0$.
例2 设 $z = x + \mathrm{i}y, y \neq 0, y \neq \pm \mathrm{i}$. 证明：当且只当 $x^{2} + y^{2} = 1$ 时，$\frac{z}{1 + z^2}$ 是实数.
证 $\frac{z}{1 + z^2}$ 是实数等价于
$$\frac {z}{1 + z ^ {2}} = \left(\overline {{\frac {z}{1 + z ^ {2}}}}\right) = \frac {\bar {z}}{1 + \bar {z} ^ {2}},$$
$$z + z \bar {z} ^ {2} = \bar {z} + \bar {z} z ^ {2},$$
亦即
$$(z - \bar {z}) (1 - z \bar {z}) = 0.$$
因 $y \neq 0$ ，故 $2\mathrm{i}y = z - \bar{z} \neq 0$ ，从而
$$1 - z \bar {z} = 0, \quad | z | ^ {2} = 1.$$
$$x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 1.$$
由于上述推导的每一步都是可逆的，故命题得证.
例3 求 $f(z) = \frac{1 + z}{1 - z}$ 的实部、虚部及模.
$$\begin{array}{r l} f (z) & = \frac {1 + z}{1 - z} = \frac {(1 + z) (1 - \bar {z})}{(1 - z) (1 - \bar {z})} \\ & = \frac {1 + z - \bar {z} - z \bar {z}}{1 - z - \bar {z} + z \bar {z}} = \frac {1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2} + i 2 y}{(1 - x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}, \end{array}$$
$$\operatorname{Re} f (z) = \frac {1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}}{(1 - x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}},$$
$$\operatorname{Im} f (z) = \frac {2 y}{(1 - x ^ {2}) + y ^ {2}}.$$
因为
$$\begin{array}{r l} | f (z) | ^ {2} & = f (z) \overline {{f (z)}} = \frac {(1 + z) (1 + \bar {z})}{(1 - z) (1 - \bar {z})} \\ & = \frac {1 + z + z + \bar {z z}}{1 - z - z + \bar {z z}} = \frac {(1 + x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}{(1 - x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}, \end{array}$$
$$\mid f (z) \mid = \sqrt {\frac {(1 + x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}{(1 - x) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}}.$$
例4 试证明实系数多项式的根共轭存在.
证 设 $z_0$ 是 $n$ 次多项式
$$p (z) = z ^ {n} + a _ {1} z ^ {n - 1} + \dots + a _ {n - 1} z + a _ {n}$$
的根，其中，各系数 $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ 都是实数。由共轭复数的性质，有
$$\begin{array}{r l} p (\bar {z} _ {0}) & = (\bar {z} _ {0}) ^ {n} + a _ {1} (\bar {z} _ {0}) ^ {n - 1} + \dots + a _ {n - 1} \bar {z} _ {0} + a _ {n} \\ & = \overline {{z _ {0} ^ {n}}} + \bar {a} _ {1} \overline {{z _ {0} ^ {n - 1}}} + \dots + \overline {{a _ {n - 1}}} \bar {z} _ {0} + \bar {a} _ {n} \\ & = \overline {{z _ {0} ^ {n}}} + a _ {1} z _ {0} ^ {n - 1} + \dots + a _ {n - 1} z _ {0} + a _ {n} = \overline {{p (z _ {0})}} = 0. \end{array}$$
这就证得  $\bar{z}_{0}$  也是  $p(z)$  的根.
例5 设 $z_{1}, z_{2}$ 为任意复数，证明
$$\left| z _ {1} + z _ {2} \right| ^ {2} = \left| z _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| z _ {2} \right| ^ {2} + 2 \operatorname{Re} \left(z _ {1} \bar {z} _ {2}\right).$$
证 由共轭复数的性质,有
$$\begin{array}{r l} | z _ {1} + z _ {2} | ^ {2} & = (z _ {1} + z _ {2}) \overline {{(z _ {1} + z _ {2})}} = (z _ {1} + z _ {2}) (\bar {z} _ {1} + \bar {z} _ {2}) \\ & = | z _ {1} | ^ {2} + | z _ {2} | ^ {2} + z _ {1} \bar {z} _ {2} + z _ {2} \bar {z} _ {1}. \end{array}$$
$$z _ {1} \dot {z} _ {2} + z _ {2} \bar {z} _ {1} = z _ {1} \bar {z} _ {2} + (\overline {{z _ {1} z _ {2}}}) = 2 \mathrm{Re} (z _ {1} \bar {z} _ {2}),$$
$$\left| z _ {1} + z _ {2} \right| ^ {2} = \left| z _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| z _ {2} \right| ^ {2} + 2 \operatorname{Re} \left(z _ {1} \bar {z} _ {2}\right).$$
