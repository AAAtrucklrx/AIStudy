### 9.2.1 线性关系

设 $f(t)$ 及 $g(t)$ 都可作拉氏变换，则对于任何两个常数 $\alpha, \beta$ 有
$$\mathrm{L} [ \alpha f (t) + \beta g (t) ] = \alpha \mathrm{L} [ f (t) ] + \beta \mathrm{L} [ g (t) ].$$
这可以由定义直接得出. 若记 $\mathrm{L}[f(t)] = F(p), \mathrm{L}[g(t)] = G(p)$, 写成反变换式就是
$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ \alpha F (p) + \beta G (p) ] & = \alpha f (t) + \beta g (t) \\ & = \alpha \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ F (p) ] + \beta \mathrm{L} ^ {- 1} [ G (p) ]. \end{array}$$
即逆变换  $L^{-1}$  也是线性的.
例3 由 $\mathbf{L}[\mathrm{e}^{at}] = \frac{1}{p - a}$，可得
$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathrm{L} [ \cos \omega t ] & = \mathrm{L} \left[ \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{i} \omega t} + \mathrm{e} ^ {- \mathrm{i} \omega t}}{2} \right] = \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{L} [ \mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{i} \omega t} ] + \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{L} [ \mathrm{e} ^ {- \mathrm{i} \omega t} ] \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {1}{p - \mathrm{i} \omega} + \frac {1}{p + \mathrm{i} \omega}\right) = \frac {p}{p ^ {2} + \omega^ {2}}. \end{array}$$
$$\mathbf {L} [ \sin \omega t ] = \frac {\omega}{p ^ {2} + \omega^ {2}};$$
$$\mathrm{L} [ \mathrm{ch} \omega t ] = \frac {p}{p ^ {2} - \omega^ {2}};$$
$$\mathrm{L} [ \mathrm{sh} _ {\omega t} ] = \frac {\omega}{p ^ {2} - \omega^ {2}}.$$
